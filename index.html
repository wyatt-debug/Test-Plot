The program

Three passes. Six quarts.

Build the biology in the fall, feed it at planting, then push the crop when it starts setting ear size.

Fall

Biomentum

3 qt/acre

Beneficial bacteria plus mycorrhizal and saprophytic fungi get to work over the winter, rebuilding the soil biology the next crop draws on.

At planting

ABC Soil Health

2 qt/acre

Aminos-N, Biomentum and humic acid in the root zone to hold nutrients where roots can reach them and drive early-season vigor.

V6

ABC Soil Health

1 qt/acre

An in-season boost right as the plant is deciding how many kernels each ear will carry.
